DPDP Act 2023 Statutory Architecture

Privacy & Clinical Data Protection

Engineered specifically for the Indian regulatory framework. Built on purpose-limited consent, cryptographic table partitioning, and an effortless mechanism to revoke access at any time.

Identity Isolation

Your login identity (email, password hash) resides in a distinct partitioned database table. Screenings, records, and journal entries reference only an opaque cryptographic UUID that cannot be reversed by external breaches.

AES-256 Envelope Encryption

Reflective free text written in your personal journal is encrypted client-to-server with AES-256-GCM. Plain text is never persisted to unencrypted disk or exposed to background analytics.

Zero Commercial Data Exploitation

MindCare AI has no third-party tracking scripts, advertising trackers, or telemetry brokers. Your mental health symptoms and scores are never traded or monetized.

Absolute Revocation & Erasure

Under Section 6(4) of the DPDP Act 2023, you maintain the fundamental right to revoke any consent purpose with the exact ease that it was granted.
